# Fool proof carbonara

Adapted from: [ethanchlebowski.com](https://www.ethanchlebowski.com/cooking-techniques-recipes/fiery-carbonara)

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes  
**Servings:** 2 persons

## Ingredients

- enough pasta for 2 persons, see [pasta in general](pastaingeneral.md) for my thoughts
- 200g thick cut (smoked) bacon / guanciale
- 4 egg yolks
- 100g Parmigiano Reggiano, grated + a little extra for dressing at the end
- 10g cornstarch
- 150g water
- lemon juice, around 1/2 Tbsp
- pinch of salt (depends on saltiness of the bacon and how salty you cook your pasta)
- some fresh ground pepper

## Instructions

### For the sauce

1. Add the cornstarch and water to a small saucepan. Mix together until dissolved. Turn the heat to low and, while whisking, bring to just below a simmer (80C). The starch should begin to thicken. Stir to ensure there are no clumps and then turn off the heat. Set aside.
2. Add the cheese, egg yolks, lemon juice, salt + pepper. Pour in the cooled cornstarch gel (so that the eggs won't cook) and blend everything with an immersion blender to create a stable emulsion of the cheese, pepper, and gel. Give it a taste, and add a touch of salt if needed. This can be stored in the fridge for 3 to 4 days.

### For the pasta

1. Fry the bacon on a medium flame in a skillet / large pan (can be done on beforehand, )
2. Set a pot of water to boil along with a big pinch of salt, cook the paste to your liking.
3. Heat off, drain the pasta and return to pot.
4. To the pasta, add the bacon and the sauce (do in steps if you are afraid of oversaucing). Slowly bring up the temperature of the sauce while creaming with the pasta (= keep stirring, don't stop). Be a little careful of the heat, but this can come to a light simmer without breaking, once you have the consistency of a droopy sauce / custard: kill the heat and serve.
5. Serve the pasta, add more grated parm and the reserved bacon / guanciale as a topping.
